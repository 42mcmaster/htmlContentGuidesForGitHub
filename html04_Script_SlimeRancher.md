# Lesson 04 Promo Script: Slime Rancher Site


---

## Script

| Seconds | Who | Line | What's on screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | NEW PLAYER | What is this thing on my back? | Figure standing with a small brick pack stuck to its back, twisting to look at it |
| 4–9 | RANCHER | A vac-pack. Suck up slimes, feed them, collect plorts. | Second figure points at the pack, then at a pink slime cutout on the ground |
| 9–14 | RANCHER | Feed one the wrong plort and it becomes a largo. Or worse. | Pink slime cutout gets swapped for a bigger two-color slime cutout |
| 14–17 | NEW PLAYER | Worse? | Figure takes a step back from the slime |
| 17–20 | RANCHER | [Your site name] explains all of it. | Both figures facing forward, site name in big text across the screen |

**Word count:** 35 with the site name (target is about 45, so you have room if you want to add a line)

**Timed reading:** about 14 seconds at a normal pace (limit is 17)

---

## Shot List

Six setups. Shoot a small handful of pictures at each one, moving things a little between each picture. Aim for 40 to 60 pictures total.

1. New Player standing alone, turning its head to look at the pack on its back
2. Rancher walks in from the left in small steps
3. Rancher's arm comes up to point at the pack, then down at the pink slime on the ground
4. Swap the pink slime cutout for the largo cutout — do it in two or three pictures so it looks like it grows
5. New Player steps backward away from the largo, one small move per picture
6. Both figures still, side by side facing forward — take extra pictures here to hold while the site name is said

---

## Props Needed

- Two Lego figures
- A small brick pack that can sit on a figure's back for the vac-pack
- **Your own slime drawings, printed and cut out.** Print the Pink slime you already drew, small enough that a figure could pick it up. Print a second, bigger one for the largo, or draw a new two-color one.
- A plain sheet of paper or a folder for the background

Using your own artwork in the video is the whole point here. Whatever ends up in your gallery table can end up in the promo.
